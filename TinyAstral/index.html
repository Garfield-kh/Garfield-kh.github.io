Abstract

Robot agents built on vision–language models depend on their harness: the tools, feedback, memory, and recovery logic around the planner. TinyAstral first freezes an open-weight ~180B teacher and improves only its harness through recursive self-improvement: frontier LLM agents propose edits, and each edit is kept only if it passes a paired statistical test. On RoboTwin this raises the teacher's success from 33.4% to 63.6%.

We then distill the teacher's trajectories, including reasoning and failure recovery, into a 4B student that replaces it in the same harness. The student reaches 62.8%, stays close to its teacher on three training benchmarks and to a 552B planner on three unseen ones, uses about one fifth of the teacher's accelerator time, and runs closed loop on a real SO-ARM101 arm served on Huawei Ascend Atlas A2.

Stage 1 · Harness RSI. LLM agents edit the frozen teacher's harness; a paired A/B test with a pre-registered gate decides what stays.
Stage 2 · Distillation. Freeze H*, then train 0.8B–4B students on teacher trajectories, reasoning and recovery included.
Deploy. The student replaces the teacher in the unchanged harness: 3 training + 3 zero-shot benchmarks, plus a real arm.
